Mic interaction

Turn on your mic.
The band starts listening.

Jam-a-Lot subtracts its own speaker sound from your mic per frequency bin, then adapts the band's intensity, phrasing, and voicings to your solo.

01

Dynamic energy tracking

Play softer and the band comes down; build and the band builds with you. Integrated frequency-selective speaker leakage suppression lets you play through room speakers.

  • Live intensity, onset density & register meters
  • Play with headphones or room speakers
02

Phrase-gap answers

Leave a gap at phrase ends and the band responds. Piano comping voicings shift register to match your pitch height and comping density.

  • Onset-based phrase-gap detection
  • Register-aware piano voicings & drum fills
03

Motif catch

Repeat a rhythmic figure twice and the drummer locks on, playing it with you through the section and resolving it cleanly into the section fill.

  • Quantized to the active feel's swing grid
  • Locks on 1-bar or 2-bar repeated figures
04

Section & chorus cues

Section boundaries are announced with hand-off fills and downbeat crashes. Chorus arrivals are clearly stated so you never lose your place in the form.

  • Phrase-locked trade re-entries on the "and of 4"
  • Distinct chorus vs section downbeat emphasis

Shape the band

Static tracks play on loop.
A band responds live.

Generated on the fly bar by bar — giving you real-time control over chorus dynamics, trading, feel switches, and reharmonization while you play.

01

Live dynamics & chorus control

Shape the next chorus without stopping. Double time into the out-head, drop to half-time for the bass solo, or dial drummer intensity (1–5) at phrase boundaries.

  • Next chorus: double time, half time, or feel switch
  • Drummer intensity: 1–5 density profiles on the fly
02

Trade bars with the drummer

Press trade to exchange fours, eights, or full choruses. The drummer solos with comping behind you and hands off with phrase-locked cymbal splashes.

  • Trade 4s / 8s / choruses with phrase alignment
  • Seamless re-entry kicks and downbeat markers
03

Live Changes reharmonization

Tasteful real-time substitutions (tritone swaps, back-door turns, turnaround variants) over recognized forms like blues and rhythm changes without mutating stored charts.

  • Substitutions display live on chart & bass line
  • 100% session-only — original chart stays untouched
04

12 Feels & instant transposition

Switch feels mid-performance, transpose ±1 semitone instantly, or loop sections until clean. Computed from absolute beat clocks with zero timing drift.

  • Swing, bossa, latin, gypsy jazz, soul-jazz, funk, blues shuffle, rock, EDM...
  • Instant semitone transposition & clean section looping

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is Jam-a-Lot really free?

Yes! During open Beta, the complete app is 100% free in exchange for your feedback. There is no credit card required and no trial timer. Guest mode lets you test 3 tunes with zero sign-up.

Do I need to install software or download samples?

No. Jam-a-Lot is completely browser-native. There are no desktop installers and zero multi-gigabyte sample packs to download. It runs smoothly on any modern desktop or tablet browser.

Can I play through room speakers, or do I need headphones?

Both work! Jam-a-Lot includes frequency-selective band leakage suppression that subtracts the backing band from your mic input in real time. You can play through room speakers or headphones seamlessly.

Can I import, edit, and export chord charts?

Yes. You can edit chord symbols with live validation, paste plain-text lead sheets, sketch original compositions, and export your generated bass lines and chord charts to MusicXML or neutral JSON formats.
