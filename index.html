5-Line Staff & AI Transcription

Read hits and melodies on the chart; transcribe rubato recordings.

Switch any chart to the 5-line Staff View to read big-band slash notation, private tune melodies, saved solo studies, or live generated bass and piano parts—all backed by one unified Staff Editor.

  • Unified Staff Editor: Author private tune melodies (on any tune), edit solo studies, or write 1–4 bar bass patterns in C that transpose across every chord root.
  • Beat-Tracked Piano Transcription: Local 6-source piano separation + Transkun/Basic Pitch (up to 3 min) tracks the band's beats so barlines follow tempo drift, with per-bar Half/Normal/Double length corrections.
  • MuseScore Shortcuts & Export: Requantize (1/8, 1/16, Swing 8ths), split/join/tie, slowed-down selection loops, and letter-size PDF or MusicXML export.

Listening band

Turn on your mic.
The band starts listening.

Jam-a-Lot subtracts its own speaker sound from your mic per frequency bin, then adapts the band's intensity, phrasing, and voicings to your solo. Record everything your microphone hears, listen back, and download the raw microphone WAV.

01 · Dynamic Calibration

One-click Set my range & Energy 0–5

Calibrate your softest and loudest playing once per mic. Play softer and the band drops down; build and the rhythm section climbs with you.

  • Speaker leakage suppression works with room speakers or headphones
  • Adjustable Response Time in seconds with a live Energy graph
02 · Conversational Comping

Phrase-gap answers

Leave a gap at phrase ends and the band responds. Piano and guitar comping voicings shift register to match your pitch height and density.

  • Onset-based phrase-gap detection
  • Register-aware voicings & conversational drum fills
03 · Rhythmic Lock

Motif catch

Repeat a rhythmic figure twice and the drummer locks on, playing it with you through the section and resolving it cleanly into the turnaround.

  • Quantized to the active feel's swing, Latin, or funk grid
  • Locks on 1-bar or 2-bar repeated figures
04 · Form Awareness

Section & chorus cues

Section boundaries are announced with jazz setup figures, melodic toms, or restrained Latin timbale and cross-stick cues so you never lose the form.

  • Phrase-locked trade re-entries on the "and of 4"
  • Distinct chorus vs. section downbeat emphasis

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is Jam-a-Lot only for jazz?

Not at all. Alongside Swing, Ballad, and Gypsy Jazz, Jam-a-Lot includes authentic Salsa, Bolero, Bolero Modern, Tresillo, Bossa Nova (with nylon-string guitar), Rio Samba batucada, Funk, Soul-Jazz, R&B / Neo-Soul, Soul Waltz, Soul 6/8, Blues Shuffle, Rock, EDM, and African/Réunion ensembles—plus a Rhythm Blend Lab to combine them.

Is Jam-a-Lot really free?

Yes. During open Beta, the complete app is 100% free in exchange for your feedback. There is no credit card required and no trial timer. Guest mode lets you test 3 tunes with zero sign-up.

Can I play through room speakers, or do I need headphones?

Both work. Jam-a-Lot includes frequency-selective band leakage suppression that subtracts the backing band from your mic input in real time, plus one-click Set my range calibration.

Can I import charts, read staff notation, and transcribe solos?

Yes. You can paste plain-text lead sheets, switch any chart to the 5-line Staff View (for rhythm hits, melodies, or bass lines), and transcribe local piano recordings onto a beat-tracked grand staff with PDF and MusicXML export.
